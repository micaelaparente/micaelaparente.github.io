Choosing a car is a tough decision. If you are not familiar with brands or  don't know the ins and outs of the automotive world, what starts with "I want a simple, reliable car to commute everyday to work" can quickly become a nightmare.

Customer reviews are a popular source of insights for people who are trying to decide which is the best option for them, but browsing through dozens of reviews, expert guides and pages on the internet can still be a lot of work.

In this project for a course from my master's, I used NLP tools such as spaCy and NLTK to analyse user reviews and make it easier for users to browse cars according to their non-technical characteristics. The result is a [very basic prototype](https://mparente.shinyapps.io/car-selector/) developed with Shiny. If you want to know more details about the process and the tools I used, feel free to check my project on [GitHub](https://github.com/micaelaparente/Capita-Selecta-Car-Selector).
